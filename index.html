Newspaper vocabulary
  • to stumble across / upon → tomber sur (conveys the accidental, lucky nature of the find; prefer over “to find”)

Historical vocabulary & prepositions of cause
  • at each crossroads / at every corner / at each junction / at every intersection → à chaque carrefour
  • les gardes mobiles → mobile police units, riot police, the CRS (Compagnies Républicaines de Sécurité) — no clean English equivalent; “mobile police” or “riot police” are the closest
  • because of / owing to / due to / on account of → à cause de
  • Historical note: les événements d’Algérie was the French government’s official euphemism for what was in fact the Algerian War of Independence (1954–1962) — “war” (guerre) was deliberately avoided.
